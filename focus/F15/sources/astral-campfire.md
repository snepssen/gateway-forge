# Focus 15 – Astral Campfire Guided Meditation

## Ocean Surf & Comfortable Position

This is the beginning of your journey toward gathering. It's a quiet, personal
path that starts in comfort and stillness.

Before we begin, take a moment to make yourself comfortable. Let your body rest
in a way that feels supported, either lying down or seated in a chair that
allows your back to relax and your shoulders to fall away from tension.

The goal isn't to fall asleep, but to become still enough that you could.

Now, you should be hearing my voice in your right ear. If you're not, turn your
headphones around so that you are.

Now, listen to the sound of the ocean surf. This is more than relaxation; this
is energy. [continues as the shared ocean segment]

## Astral Campfire Arrival

You find yourself in the quiet void of the starfield of reality. In this
exercise, you will light a campfire — an invitation to any individuals equal to
or greater than yourself, who may wish to join you.

The fireplace is prepared before you, the wood neatly stacked and waiting.
Kneel down and see the small box of matches lying there for you. Slide it open.
Take a match. And light it.

Hear the sharp scratch, see the flare of light. Guide the flame into the
waiting timber. Lean close, blow gently, and watch as the small spark grows
into a steady fire.

The campfire is now lit — a beacon of presence and connection.

Feel the warmth of the flames brushing against your skin. Hear the gentle
crackle and pop as the wood begins to burn. Notice the faint smell of smoke
curling into the night air. Watch how the firelight flickers and bends, casting
soft shadows that move across the ground.

When you feel the fire burning steadily, take your place nearby. Sit
comfortably, and let its glow settle you here and now.

The beacon is lit. You are present. Now let others approach in their own time.

## Calling / Signaling

As you sit, breathe into your chest. On the exhale, let the light of the
campfire brighten, just a little.

You are sending a signal — not with words, but with presence.

Say inwardly: "I am here. I welcome connection."

Your flame glows softly in answer. Others may see it, feel it, sense it. Let
this signal radiate out into the surrounding area.

## Group Presence

Now that the fire burns steadily, sit with it. You have made yourself visible.
You are here.

Simply wait.

If contact comes, you will feel it. Not as a thought, but as a presence. It
will draw your attention in a way you cannot ignore.

Like a shout without volume. Like a single footstep in silence. It may feel
like a tension in the space, a weight that wasn't there before.

You may sense where it comes from, just as you know where a sound originates
even when you cannot see it.

This is not about imagining, or trying to know. It is about noticing what
arrives on its own.

Rest here, with the fire before you, until the experience feels complete.

[30 minutes of silence for connection]

## Gratitude & Closure

Before you depart, take a moment to acknowledge those who joined you. Offer
thanks for their presence, in whatever way feels natural. No words are
necessary — intent is enough.

Now, turn your attention back to the fire. Watch as the flames begin to fold in
upon themselves. The light dims, the heat fades, and the entire fireplace
resets.

The void absorbs it back into nothingness. In its place, a new unlit fireplace
forms — clear, and untouched. It shimmers for a moment like a mirage, then
ripples away into silence.

The space is reset, and left just as you found it.
