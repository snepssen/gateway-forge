The void proper. Completely dark — no starlight, no dots, no features of any
kind.

The white dot is not in F26. You glance it only on departure, at the edge of the
transition into F27: it grows, brightens, expands to meet you, and you pass
through it and end up in the Park.

**Diverges from the published text, and not by a little.** The Monroe Institute
places F26 inside the Belief System Territories (24–26): "certain areas, few in
number, containing the structures and knowledge of various highly individual
religious and other beliefs based on direct experience of self." Nothing like
that has been found here. Both readings are kept — see `published` in
`levels.json` — because the disagreement is the point, not a bug to reconcile.

**A third account, from the primary corpus.** "Intro Focus 27" (Wave VII -
Voyager, CD2-4) passes through F26 on the way from 25 to 27 and describes it
in passing, not as a destination: "the pattern of 25 is fading, more and
more... but the light remains." Neither the found darkness nor the published
Belief System Territories — a third, thin thing, kept beside the other two
rather than picked over them. Voiced in no segment; briefing-f26.gws stays
with the found account, which is the fuller and better-attested of the two
the user can act on.
